<h1 class="contract">createpoll</h1>

---
spec-version: "0.2.0"
title: Create poll.
summary: 'Creates a poll to allow signed up users to vote on.'
icon: https://wire.network/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/WIRE_Icon_White_min.png
---

<b>Description:</b>
<div class="description">
Creates a poll that users can participate with voting in. The creator will decide the name, description, and options.
</div>

<b>Clauses:</b>
<div class="clauses">
Only 1 vote per user per poll. Creator of poll can vote on poll as well.
</div>
